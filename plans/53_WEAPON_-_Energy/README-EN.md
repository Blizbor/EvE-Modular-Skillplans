# 53 WEAPON - Energy

[🇵🇱 Polski](README-PL.md) | 🇬🇧 **English**

**Category:** WEAPON

## Purpose

A modular plan for **Energy** competency. Train it only when the selected hull or doctrine actually uses it.

> **Milestone:** BASIC = small T2, IMPROVED = medium T2, STANDARD = large T2 + developed specialization.

## Levels

### 1 — BASIC

The lowest sensible entry level. It should make the competency usable without delaying the pilot with unnecessary training.

Import: [`plan-1-BASIC.txt`](plan-1-BASIC.txt)

Target levels:

- Gunnery IV
- Jury Rigging III
- Energy Weapon Rigging I
- Small Energy Turret V
- Small Pulse Laser Specialization III
- Small Beam Laser Specialization III

### 2 — IMPROVED

The intermediate practical level. This is often a good point for regular use before deciding whether the role deserves more SP.

Import: [`plan-2-IMPROVED.txt`](plan-2-IMPROVED.txt)

Target levels:

- Gunnery IV
- Controlled Bursts III

### 3 — STANDARD

The highest current library level — the **corp standard** for a pilot developing this competency. It does not mean automatically training everything to V.

## Controlled Bursts

`Controlled Bursts` remains part of the **Hybrid** and **Energy** paths because it reduces turret capacitor use by 5% per level. It does not gate BASIC: entry progression prioritizes unlocking the weapon system. IMPROVED targets III and STANDARD targets IV. Level V is a fit-specific decision for cap-sensitive setups.

## Level V rule

Level V stays in a plan when it unlocks a required module/hull/skill, is required for further progression, or is an intentional role-specific exception. After reaching `STANDARD`, further V levels are the pilot's decision rather than an automatic requirement.
