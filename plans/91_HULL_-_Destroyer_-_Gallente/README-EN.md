# 91 HULL - Destroyer - Gallente

[🇵🇱 Polski](README-PL.md) | 🇬🇧 **English**

**Category:** HULL

## Purpose

A modular hull-progression plan for **Destroyer - Gallente**. Combine it with the appropriate tank, weapon and support plans through `training-paths/`.

## Levels

### 1 — BASIC

The lowest sensible entry level. It should make the competency usable without delaying the pilot with unnecessary training.

Import: [`plan-1-BASIC.txt`](plan-1-BASIC.txt)

Target levels:

- Spaceship Command I
- Gallente Frigate III
- Gallente Destroyer III

### 2 — IMPROVED

The intermediate practical level. This is often a good point for regular use before deciding whether the role deserves more SP.

Import: [`plan-2-IMPROVED.txt`](plan-2-IMPROVED.txt)

Target levels:

- Spaceship Command I
- Gallente Frigate III
- Gallente Destroyer IV

### 3 — STANDARD

The highest current library level — the **corp standard** for a pilot developing this competency. It does not mean automatically training everything to V.

Import: [`plan-3-STANDARD.txt`](plan-3-STANDARD.txt)

Target levels:

- Spaceship Command I
- Gallente Frigate III
- Gallente Destroyer V
- Gallente Tactical Destroyer IV

## Level V rule

Level V stays in a plan when it unlocks a required module/hull/skill, is required for further progression, or is an intentional role-specific exception. After reaching `STANDARD`, further V levels are the pilot's decision rather than an automatic requirement.
