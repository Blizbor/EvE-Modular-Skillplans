# 40 LOGISTICS - Remote Armor Repair

[🇵🇱 Polski](README-PL.md) | 🇬🇧 **English**

**Category:** LOGISTICS

## Purpose

Remote armor repair capacitor efficiency and T2 remote armor repairer access.

> **Milestone:** BASIC unlocks small T2 remote armor repairers; IMPROVED unlocks medium and large T2 remote armor repairers; STANDARD is the dedicated specialist level.
