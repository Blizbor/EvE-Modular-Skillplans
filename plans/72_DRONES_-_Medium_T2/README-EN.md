# 72 DRONES - Medium T2

[🇵🇱 Polski](README-PL.md) | 🇬🇧 **English**

**Category:** WEAPON

## Purpose

A modular plan for **Medium T2** competency. Train it only when the selected hull or doctrine actually uses it.

> **Milestone:** IMPROVED is the practical T2 medium-drone access milestone; STANDARD develops specialization further.

## Levels

### Alpha — 1.5

An Alpha pilot completes BASIC but not all of IMPROVED. The file contains every entry from the STANDARD plan that fits within Alpha limits.

Import: [`plan-1.5-ALPHA.txt`](plan-1.5-ALPHA.txt)

Maximum Alpha-accessible levels in this module:

- Drones V
- Medium Drone Operation IV
- Amarr Drone Specialization II
- Caldari Drone Specialization II
- Gallente Drone Specialization II
- Minmatar Drone Specialization II

### 1 — BASIC

The lowest sensible entry level. It should make the competency usable without delaying the pilot with unnecessary training.

Import: [`plan-1-BASIC.txt`](plan-1-BASIC.txt)

Target levels:

- Drones V
- Medium Drone Operation IV

### 2 — IMPROVED

The intermediate practical level. This is often a good point for regular use before deciding whether the role deserves more SP.

Import: [`plan-2-IMPROVED.txt`](plan-2-IMPROVED.txt)

Target levels:

- Drones V
- Medium Drone Operation V
- Amarr Drone Specialization II
- Caldari Drone Specialization II
- Gallente Drone Specialization II
- Minmatar Drone Specialization II

### 3 — STANDARD

The highest current library level — the **corp standard** for a pilot developing this competency. It does not mean automatically training everything to V.

Import: [`plan-3-STANDARD.txt`](plan-3-STANDARD.txt)

Target levels:

- Drones V
- Medium Drone Operation V
- Amarr Drone Specialization IV
- Caldari Drone Specialization IV
- Gallente Drone Specialization IV
- Minmatar Drone Specialization IV

## Level V rule

Level V stays in a plan when it unlocks a required module/hull/skill, is required for further progression, or is an intentional role-specific exception. After reaching `STANDARD`, further V levels are the pilot's decision rather than an automatic requirement.
