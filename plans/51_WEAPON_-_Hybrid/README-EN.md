**Category:** WEAPON

## Purpose

A modular plan for **Hybrid** competency. Train it only when the selected hull or doctrine actually uses it.

> **Milestone:** BASIC = small T2, IMPROVED = medium T2, STANDARD = large T2 + developed specialization.

## Controlled Bursts

`Controlled Bursts` remains part of the **Hybrid** and **Energy** paths because it reduces turret capacitor use by 5% per level. It does not gate BASIC: entry progression prioritizes unlocking the weapon system. IMPROVED targets III and STANDARD targets IV. Level V is a fit-specific decision for cap-sensitive setups.

## Level V rule

Level V stays in a plan when it unlocks a required module/hull/skill, is required for further progression, or is an intentional role-specific exception. After reaching `STANDARD`, further V levels are the pilot's decision rather than an automatic requirement.
