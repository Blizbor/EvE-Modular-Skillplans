# 80 SCOUT - Signature, Combat & Covert

[🇵🇱 Polski](README-PL.md) | 🇬🇧 **English**

**Category:** SCOUT

## Goal

BASIC should **unlock the gameplay area with the smallest sensible SP investment**. Level III is a useful default minimum for many skills, but there are deliberate exceptions in both directions: sometimes I is enough, while some capabilities genuinely require IV/V.

For scanning, `Astrometrics` has priority. Each level improves probe strength, maximum deviation and scan time at the same time. `Astrometrics V` also unlocks T2 probe launchers.

`Astrometric Acquisition` is intentionally omitted at all three levels: it only improves scan time and its ROI is too low compared with probe strength and deviation reduction.

## Levels

### Alpha — 1.5

An Alpha pilot completes BASIC but not all of IMPROVED. The file contains every entry from the STANDARD plan that fits within Alpha limits.

Import: [`plan-1.5-ALPHA.txt`](plan-1.5-ALPHA.txt)

Maximum Alpha-accessible levels in this module:

- Science III
- Astrometrics III
- Astrometric Rangefinding II
- CPU Management IV
- Power Grid Management II
- Electronics Upgrades V
- Spaceship Command III

### 1 — BASIC
Import: [`plan-1-BASIC.txt`](plan-1-BASIC.txt)
- Science III
- **Astrometrics III**
- no Rangefinding
- no Pinpointing
- no Acquisition

This is the true entry level: the pilot can scan and enter exploration without spending SP on support skills that do not unlock a new gameplay area yet.

### 2 — IMPROVED
Import: [`plan-2-IMPROVED.txt`](plan-2-IMPROVED.txt)
- Science III
- **Astrometrics IV**
- Astrometric Rangefinding II
- Astrometric Pinpointing II
- no Acquisition

Support skills begin here. Pinpointing requires Astrometrics IV, making this a natural mechanical threshold.

### 3 — STANDARD
Import: [`plan-3-STANDARD.txt`](plan-3-STANDARD.txt)
- **Astrometrics V**
- Astrometric Rangefinding III
- Astrometric Pinpointing III
- no Acquisition
- CPU Management IV
- Power Grid Management II
- Electronics Upgrades V
- Spaceship Command III
- Cloaking IV
- Covert Ops IV

`Astrometrics V` has unusually high ROI: it gives all three scanning bonuses at once and unlocks T2 probe launchers. Rangefinding/Pinpointing stop at III here; Acquisition remains optional.
