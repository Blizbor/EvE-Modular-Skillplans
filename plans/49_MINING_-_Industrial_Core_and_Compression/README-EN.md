# 49 MINING - Industrial Core and Compression

[🇵🇱 Polski](README-PL.md) | 🇬🇧 **English**

**Category:** MINING

## Purpose

Subcapital industrial cores, shipboard compressors and fleet compression range.

> **Milestone:** BASIC unlocks a medium or large Industrial Core I and asteroid-ore compression; IMPROVED unlocks Medium Industrial Core II plus ice and gas compression; STANDARD covers Large Industrial Core II and all subcapital compression types.

## Levels

- Leadership III

## Level V rule

Level V remains when it unlocks a required module or skill, or provides an unusually important role-specific benefit. Further V levels remain the pilot's decision.
