# EMS Homefront Operations — farm squads based on EVE University / HF.C

# 1. Purpose

The priority of this document is **practical Homefront Operations farming**:

- which squad to bring;
- which exact fits to use;
- how to adapt tank and damage to the empire region;
- where a simpler or more accessible fleet variant exists.

What the pilot or fleet learns along the way is a secondary benefit. Homefronts are excellent practice for logistics, cap chains, target priority, tackle, hacking and operating under pressure, but the first goal here is **repeatable, safe and reasonably efficient site completion**.

The baseline is the current HF.C fitting set published on EVE University after the March 2026 Homefront changes.

HF.C also publishes Kikimora variants. This document **excludes the Kikimora from recommended squads** because it requires a separate and relatively specialized Triglavian hull/weapon skill branch. The fit remains available in the EVE University source.

Regional sections use empire names directly: **Amarr, Caldari, Gallente, Minmatar**.

# 5. Three-pilot Homefronts

## 5.1 Salvage Research

### Farming squad

**Simplest:**

`3x HFC SR Punisher MWD`

**More margin on one pilot:**

`2x HFC SR Punisher MWD + 1x HFC SR Punisher AB`

**Fast but more fragile alternative:**

`3x HFC SR Heron`

HF.C describes the MWD Punisher as fast, the AB Punisher as very tanky, and the Heron as fast but more fragile.

The pilot holding aggro pulls NPCs away and can work the outer wrecks. Pilots without aggro salvage closer to the NPC group.

There is one version of the site across New Eden, so the fit does not need regional variants.

**Training:** aggro splitting, movement and capacitor management without trying to solve the site with more DPS.

---

## 5.2 Stabilize Rift

### Farming squad

**Standard:**

`3x HFC StaR Dragoon`

**More expensive variant:**

`2x HFC StaR Dragoon + 1x HFC StaR Cruor`

**Low-SP:**

`3x HFC StaR Dragoon Low SP`

The entire site is built around capacitor warfare. Neutralize the structures, quickly remove NPCs that restore capacitor, and react to additional neut pressure.

There is one version of the site across New Eden, so ammo and tank do not need regional changes.

**Training:** capacitor warfare, managing your own cap while neuts run continuously, and mechanical target priority.

---

## 5.3 Traffic Stop

### Farming squad

**Recommended:**

`2x HFC TS DD Thrasher + 1x HFC TS Support Dragoon`

**Laser alternative:**

`2x HFC TS DD Coercer T2 + 1x HFC TS Support Dragoon`

The Thrasher cargo-scans the hauler and kills only the contraband target. The Support Dragoon clears Phaseless Cutters, assists with scanning and collects loot.

Escorts always deal EM damage in this site. Hauler resist profiles vary by empire.

### Regional variants

| Region | Hauler weakness | T1 missile | T1 projectile | T1 drones | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Amarr** | Explosive / Kinetic | `Nova` / `Scourge` | `Fusion` / `Titanium Sabot` | `Warrior / Valkyrie / Berserker` or `Hornet / Vespa / Wasp` | Thrasher benefits strongly from regional damage selection; Coercer cannot select Ex/Kin |
| **Caldari** | EM / Explosive | `Mjolnir` / `Nova` | `EMP` / `Fusion` | `Acolyte / Infiltrator / Praetor` or `Warrior / Valkyrie / Berserker` | prefer EM against haulers |
| **Gallente** | EM / Explosive | `Mjolnir` / `Nova` | `EMP` / `Fusion` | `Acolyte / Infiltrator / Praetor` or `Warrior / Valkyrie / Berserker` | prefer EM against haulers |
| **Minmatar** | EM / Thermal | `Mjolnir` / `Inferno` | `EMP` / `Phased Plasma` | `Acolyte / Infiltrator / Praetor` or `Hobgoblin / Hammerhead / Ogre` | prefer EM against haulers |

Phaseless Cutters are weak to Explosive/Kinetic regardless of region. Because the primary job of `HFC TS Support Dragoon` is killing Cutters, `Nova` and `Warrior` remain sensible even when the Thrashers change ammunition for the regional haulers.

**Training:** target discipline — scan first, fire second.

# 6. Five-pilot Homefronts

## 6.0 Regional conversion and fitting

If you change only the resistance damage type within **the same module family and the same meta/tech level**, the Powergrid and CPU requirements of the fits remain the same.

| Module family | CPU | PG |
|---|---:|---:|
| Compact damage-specific shield hardener | 30 tf | 1 MW |
| Enduring damage-specific shield hardener | 40 tf | 1 MW |
| T2 damage-specific shield hardener | 44 tf | 1 MW |
| Compact damage-specific shield amplifier | 20 tf | 1 MW |
| Experimental Enduring damage-specific armor hardener | 33 tf | 1 MW |
| T2 damage-specific armor hardener | 36 tf | 1 MW |

Resistance rigs of the same size and tech level can be changed between EM/Thermal/Kinetic/Explosive without changing ship CPU/PG.

Ammunition and drones also do not change fitting.

T1 ammunition naming used in the tables:
- EM missile: `Mjolnir`
- Thermal missile: `Inferno`
- Kinetic missile: `Scourge`
- Explosive missile: `Nova`
- EM projectile: `EMP`
- Thermal projectile: `Phased Plasma`
- Kinetic-oriented projectile: `Titanium Sabot`
- Explosive projectile: `Fusion`

Hybrid turrets remain Kinetic/Thermal and lasers remain EM/Thermal. On those platforms, regional damage selection comes primarily from drones or from choosing a different weapon platform.

## 6.1 Dread Assault

### Farming squad

**Armor:**

`4x HFC DA Red Logi Augoror + 1x HFC DA Red DD Vexor`

**Armor, faster:**

`3x HFC DA Red Logi Augoror + 2x HFC DA Red DD Vexor`

HF.C generally recommends 3–4 logistics ships and 1–2 DD. The 4+1 setup has more margin; 3+2 provides more DPS.

### Regional variants

| Region | Incoming | EWAR | Weakness | T1 missile | T1 projectile | T1 drones for weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Amarr** | EM / Thermal | Neuts, weapon disruption, ECM | Explosive | `Nova` | `Fusion` | `Warrior / Valkyrie / Berserker` |
| **Caldari** | Explosive / EM | Neuts, ECM | Thermal | `Inferno` | `Phased Plasma` | `Hobgoblin / Hammerhead / Ogre` |
| **Gallente** | Explosive / EM | Neuts, ECM | Thermal | `Inferno` | `Phased Plasma` | `Hobgoblin / Hammerhead / Ogre` |
| **Minmatar** | Explosive / Kinetic | Paints, ECM | EM | `Mjolnir` | `EMP` | `Acolyte / Infiltrator / Praetor` |

### Tank changes from the baseline Minmatar fit

`HFC DA Red Logi Augoror` and `HFC DA Red DD Vexor`:
- Minmatar: Explosive + Kinetic;
- Caldari/Gallente: Explosive + EM;
- Amarr: EM + Thermal.

These changes do not alter CPU or Powergrid requirements.

`HFC DA Red DD Vexor` uses railguns, so hybrid ammunition cannot cleanly switch to EM or Explosive. **Use drones as the primary regional damage selector** according to the table.

**Training:** cap-chain discipline and identifying which NPCs are actually slowing the objective.

## 6.2 Emergency Aid

### Farming squad

**Shield:**

`4x HFC EA Red Shield - Logi Osprey + 1x HFC EA Red Shield - DD Belli`

**Armor:**

`4x HFC EA Red Logi Augoror + 1x HFC EA Red DD Vexor`

Shield is a clear baseline with the Bellicose as dedicated DD. Armor is an alternative for pilots with stronger Amarr/Gallente armor-logistics skills.

### Regional variants

| Region | Incoming | EWAR | Weakness | T1 missile | T1 projectile | T1 drones for weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Amarr** | Thermal / Explosive | ECM | EM | `Mjolnir` | `EMP` | `Acolyte / Infiltrator / Praetor` |
| **Caldari** | Kinetic / Thermal | ECM | EM | `Mjolnir` | `EMP` | `Acolyte / Infiltrator / Praetor` |
| **Gallente** | EM / Thermal | Webs | Explosive | `Nova` | `Fusion` | `Warrior / Valkyrie / Berserker` |
| **Minmatar** | Explosive / Kinetic | Paints | EM | `Mjolnir` | `EMP` | `Acolyte / Infiltrator / Praetor` |

### Shield tank

`HFC EA Red Shield - Logi Osprey`:
- Minmatar: 2x Explosive + 2x Kinetic;
- Caldari: 2x Kinetic + 2x Thermal;
- Gallente: 2x EM + 2x Thermal;
- Amarr: 2x Thermal + 2x Explosive.

`HFC EA Red Shield - DD Belli`:
- use one hardener for each incoming damage type;
- change missiles according to the table: `Mjolnir` or `Nova`.

### Armor tank

`HFC EA Red Logi Augoror` and `HFC EA Red DD Vexor`:
- Minmatar: Explosive + Kinetic;
- Caldari: Kinetic + Thermal;
- Gallente: EM + Thermal;
- Amarr: Thermal + Explosive.

Swapping to the equivalent hardeners at the same tier does not change CPU or Powergrid requirements.

HF.C often keeps Caldari Navy/Hornet drones for shield buffer and simplicity. If pure DPS is the priority, the table shows the drone family matching the regional weakness.

**Training:** cap chain and remote repairs without losing application on the DD side.

## 6.4 Raid

### Farming squad

**Shield:**

`1x HFC Raid Red Logi Osprey Ω + 1x HFC Raid Red Hauler CH2 Badger + 3x HFC Raid Red DD Caracal Mk.II`

This is the clearest role split: one logi, one hauler, three tackled DD ships.

After the March 2026 rebalance, the fleet must deliver 4 objective items. `HFC Raid Red Hauler CH2 Badger` is built to carry two at a time.

### Regional variants

| Region | Incoming | EWAR | Weakness | T1 missile | T1 projectile | T1 drones for weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Amarr** | EM / Thermal | Neuts, weapon disruption | Explosive | `Nova` | `Fusion` | `Warrior / Valkyrie / Berserker` |
| **Caldari** | Kinetic / Thermal | ECM | EM | `Mjolnir` | `EMP` | `Acolyte / Infiltrator / Praetor` |
| **Gallente** | Thermal / Kinetic | Damps | EM | `Mjolnir` | `EMP` | `Acolyte / Infiltrator / Praetor` |
| **Minmatar** | Thermal / EM | ECM | Explosive | `Nova` | `Fusion` | `Warrior / Valkyrie / Berserker` |

### Exact changes from the baseline Minmatar fit

**Caldari / Gallente**
- `HFC Raid Red Logi Osprey Ω`: EM hardener to Kinetic; EM resistance rig to Kinetic; keep Thermal;
- `HFC Raid Red DD Caracal Mk.II`: EM shield amplifier to Kinetic; EM rig to Kinetic; `Nova` to `Mjolnir`;
- `HFC Raid Red Hauler CH2 Badger`: Enduring EM hardener to Enduring Kinetic; keep Thermal and multispectrum.

**Amarr**
- the Minmatar baseline EM/Thermal tank already matches the required resistance family;
- Caracals continue to use `Nova` because the weakness is Explosive.

These module changes do not alter CPU or Powergrid requirements.

**Training:** tackle as a PvE tool and coordinating kill/loot timing.

## 6.5 Suspicious Signal

### Farming squad

**Shield:**

`1x HFC SuS Red Logi Osprey + 3x HFC SuS Red Hacker Vexor + 1x HFC SuS Red DD Vexor`

A faster variant can fit a Data Analyzer on the Osprey logi. One of the three `HFC SuS Red Hacker Vexor` ships can then be replaced by a second `HFC SuS Red DD Vexor`, a Vexor configured primarily to burn the objective structure during the vulnerability window. This increases vulnerability-window DPS but places much more workload on the logi pilot.

### Regional variants

| Region | Incoming | EWAR | NPC weakness | T1 missile | T1 projectile | T1 drones for weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Amarr** | Explosive / Kinetic | Paints | EM | `Mjolnir` | `EMP` | `Acolyte / Infiltrator / Praetor` |
| **Caldari** | Kinetic / Thermal | Damps | EM | `Mjolnir` | `EMP` | `Acolyte / Infiltrator / Praetor` |
| **Gallente** | Kinetic / Thermal | ECM | EM | `Mjolnir` | `EMP` | `Acolyte / Infiltrator / Praetor` |
| **Minmatar** | EM / Thermal | Neuts, weapon disruption | Explosive | `Nova` | `Fusion` | `Warrior / Valkyrie / Berserker` |

### Tank

`HFC SuS Red Logi Osprey`, `HFC SuS Red Hacker Vexor` and `HFC SuS Red DD Vexor` use the Minmatar baseline:
- Minmatar: EM + Thermal;
- Caldari/Gallente: Kinetic + Thermal;
- Amarr: Explosive + Kinetic.

Changing hardeners within the same module family and meta/tech level does not change CPU or Powergrid requirements. Change resistance rigs together with the tank profile.

The Vexors use hybrid turrets, so the guns cannot select arbitrary damage types. **Use drones as the primary regional NPC-damage selector.**

The objective tower has no resistances, so regional weakness matters mainly for NPC control rather than for tower burst.

**Training:** synchronizing hacks, concise ready checks and maximizing the short vulnerability window.

## 6.6 Abyssal Artifact Recovery

AAR has one version across New Eden. There are no Amarr/Caldari/Gallente/Minmatar variants.

### HFC — spider Vexor

**Shield spider:**

`2x HFC AAR Duet - Pioneer T2 + 3x HFC AAR Spider Vexor`

### HFC — spider Arbitrator

**Shield spider:**

`2x HFC AAR Duet - Pioneer T2 + 3x HFC AAR Spider Arbitrator`

The spider droneboats combine remote reps, cap chain and drone DPS. At least one should be ready to feed capacitor to a neuted Pioneer.

### EMS — 3 Pioneer + 2 Osprey

**Shield logi:**

`3x HFC AAR Duet - Pioneer T2 + 2x HFC AAR Duet - Logi Osprey`

**This is not an EVE University / HF.C doctrine.**

The **3 Pioneer + 2 Osprey** composition comes from the community concept of three miners supported by two Ospreys. EMS does not claim the composition itself; the EMS work here is mainly the fitting progression from T1/Alpha toward relaxed regular T2/Omega farming, plus clearer role and requirement documentation.

Farm-first advantages:
- the third Pioneer increases mining margin;
- two dedicated Ospreys are simpler to execute than a spider tank;
- roles are explicit: three miners finish the objective, two logi keep the fleet alive.

Trade-off:
- less dedicated droneboat DPS than `2 Pioneer + 3 Spider Vexor`.

Before treating it as equally validated as the HFC baseline, it should be live-tested over a meaningful number of runs.

**Training:** mining-objective coordination and conventional shield logistics.
