# T1 Frigate, Destroyer, Cruiser and T2 HAC

[🇵🇱 Polski](README-PL.md) | 🇬🇧 **English**

These documents describe the complete pilot progression: T1 Frigate, T1 Destroyer, T1 Cruiser and T2 Heavy Assault Cruiser. Every stage is a useful stopping point, shows which tools are being developed and explains what the next SP investment provides. Alpha pilots can complete the T1 stages but cannot fly Heavy Assault Cruisers.

## How this path develops

| Stage | Transitional level | Regular-use level | What the next stage provides |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Frigate | racial Frigate III (`90 B`) | racial Frigate IV (`90 I`) | stronger hull bonuses; level V is only needed for the relevant T2 hulls |
| T1 Destroyer | racial Destroyer III (`91 B`) | racial Destroyer IV (`91 I`) | stronger hull bonuses; level V only for selected T2/T3 destroyers |
| T1 Cruiser | racial Cruiser III (`92 B`) | racial Cruiser IV (`92 I`) | the practical threshold for regular cruiser use |
| HAC | HAC III (`97 ... B`) | HAC IV (`97 ... I`) | another level of every HAC bonus on an expensive T2 hull |

Frigate III, Destroyer III and Cruiser III are valid unlock or first-trial stages. When a pilot intends to fly a T1 hull regularly, level IV is the natural target. Moving to a HAC requires racial Cruiser V, but does not require training the destroyer hull to V.

## How to read the faction maps

- Blue nodes show shared hull progression and the skills required to open the next class.
- Separate weapon and drone chains show which earlier skills remain useful on a specific ship.
- Identical hull requirements do not imply identical weapons. For example, Zealot and Sacrilege both require Amarr Cruiser V, but one develops lasers and the other missiles.
- Tank is selected for the hull and actual fit. A shared faction or hull class is not a reason to train every tank branch at once.
- Drones are the primary weapon only in the marked branch. On other hulls they may be secondary and do not require completing the full specialization before the ship is used.

## Support progression

- BASIC support is enough to begin learning a cheap frigate and its weapon system.
- On a destroyer, completing the selected small-weapon branch matters more than raising every unrelated support module.
- A cruiser should have CORE, fitting, tank and the selected weapon at IMPROVED; `10 S` supplies Weapon Upgrades V and Advanced Weapon Upgrades IV.
- Before a HAC, prioritize racial Cruiser V, HAC IV, T2 medium weapons and the tank used by the actual fit. STANDARD support is specialization, not an automatic requirement.

- [Amarr](amarr/README-EN.md) — armor ships using energy turrets, drones or missiles; branches end at Zealot or Sacrilege.
- [Caldari](caldari/README-EN.md) — shield ships using missiles or hybrid turrets; branches end at Cerberus or Eagle.
- [Gallente](gallente/README-EN.md) — armor ships using hybrid turrets or drones; branches end at Deimos or Ishtar.
- [Minmatar](minmatar/README-EN.md) — shield or armor ships using projectile turrets or missiles; branches end at Vagabond or Muninn.
