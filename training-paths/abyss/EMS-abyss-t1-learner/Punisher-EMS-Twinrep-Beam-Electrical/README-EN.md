# Punisher EMS Twinrep Beam Electrical

**Role in the path:** stage 1, fundamentals with a large tank margin.  
**Activity:** solo T0 Electrical  
**Fit:** [FIT.eft](FIT.eft)

## Design decisions

- **Dual rep** is a training tool, giving time to notice bad range, ammo or target-priority decisions.
- `Damage Control II` is replaced by `Multispectrum Coating II`; this role tanks armor, and the recovered CPU enables the complete T2 layout.
- `Small Capacitor Control Circuit I` recovers part of the capacitor support lost with the battery.
- The battery is replaced by `Optical Compact Tracking Computer`, turning range/application into a pilot decision.
- Four `Small Focused Beam Laser II` keep the weapon system aligned with the later Amarr beam/armor line.

## How to fly it

Treat dual rep as reserve, not as two permanent toggles. Practice crystal changes, TC scripts, staggered repairs, capacitor control and target priority. If T0 damage regularly reaches hull, fix piloting before adding emergency buffer.
