# EMS Abyss T1 Learner

`EMS-abyss-t1-learner` is not an ISK/h optimization path and not a catalogue of “best farming fits”.

Its purpose is to **teach the pilot how to fly in the Abyss** before solo T1 becomes routine. T0 Electrical is used as a training ground: forgiving enough to practice, but still demanding correct range control, capacitor management, application, tank management and target priority.

The fits can be used for regular T0 runs, but each next ship deliberately removes part of the previous safety margin and adds new pilot workload.

## Intended pilot

These are **not Day1 fits**.

The path assumes some character preparation: T2 small lasers, basic gunnery support, Weapon Upgrades IV, solid fitting skills and active armor skills. Alpha can follow the whole path within Alpha limits; Omega gains a stronger hull through Amarr Frigate V and stronger support skills.

## Pilot progression

| Stage | Fit | Main lesson | What changes |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Punisher EMS Twinrep Beam Electrical](Punisher-EMS-Twinrep-Beam-Electrical/README-EN.md) | beam basics, crystal choice, tracking-computer use, capacitor, repairs and target priority | largest error margin through dual rep |
| 2 | [Tormentor EMS 1Rep Beam Electrical](Tormentor-EMS-1Rep-Beam-Electrical/README-EN.md) | application, two tracking computers, drones and greater dependence on speed/range | second repairer disappears; drones and a second TC are added |
| 3 | [Executioner EMS Beam MWD Samurai Electrical](Executioner-EMS-Beam-MWD-Samurai-Electrical/README-EN.md) | manual range control, MWD pulsing, signature bloom and angular/transversal | smallest tank margin; MWD becomes the main positioning tool |

The order is deliberate. Each next fit is not necessarily a faster T0 farmer. It requires the pilot to do more of the work that earlier stages allowed the tank to absorb.

## Completion criteria

The path is complete when the pilot can consistently manage range without mindless `Orbit`, change crystals and tracking scripts deliberately, distinguish paper DPS from application, manage repair/cap proactively, control drones without losing ship awareness, use MWD as a positioning tool and recognize when incoming damage is caused by piloting rather than by a weak fit.

Only then should the pilot move to a separate solo T1 path. Retribution is the natural later continuation of the Amarr beam/armor line, but it is not part of this document.
