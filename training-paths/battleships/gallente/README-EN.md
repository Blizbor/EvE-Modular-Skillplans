# GALLENTE — Battleship training path

[🇵🇱 Polski](README-PL.md) | 🇬🇧 **English**

This document does **not recommend one hull over another**. It shows the sensible T1 battleship options available in the race, their roles, and the modular skill branches they use.

## Common foundation

`00 → 01 → 02 → 10`, plus `03` for serious PvP. BASIC is enough for first tests; IMPROVED is the normal regular-use hull milestone; STANDARD is specialization.

## Available hulls

- **Dominix** — drone battleship; drones + armor according to fit.
- **Megathron** — hybrid turret DPS; hybrids + armor.
- **Hyperion** — hybrid turrets + strong local armor repair bonus.

### Dominix

`00 I → 01 I → 02 I → 10 S → 22 I → 70 I → 73 → 94 Gallente B/I`

Large turret training is not mandatory when the fit is drone-centered.

### Megathron

`00 I → 01 I → 02 I → 10 S → 21/22 I → 50 I → 51 → 94 Gallente B/I`

Large Hybrid IV can be an entry point; T2 large hybrids belong to the regular committed path.

### Hyperion

`00 I → 01 I → 02 I → 10 S → 22 I → 50 I → 51 → 94 Gallente B/I`

The local-repair bonus makes the active-armor branch especially relevant when the fit is built around that bonus.
