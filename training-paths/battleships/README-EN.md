# Battleships

[🇵🇱 Polski](README-PL.md) | 🇬🇧 **English**

Each racial document presents sensible T1 Battleships, their roles and the module branches they use. It does not select a hull for the reader. The choice depends on the activity and exact fit.

## Progression thresholds

| Area | First trials | Regular use | Specialization |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hull | racial Battleship III (`94 B`) | racial Battleship IV (`94 I`) | Battleship V (`94 S`) for a frequently used hull or later T2 hulls |
| CORE and fitting | BASIC only in a cheap, proven fit | IMPROVED | STANDARD only when required by fitting or specialization |
| Tank | BASIC as an entry stage | IMPROVED matching the fit | STANDARD for demanding roles, not automatically |
| Large weapon | meta/faction at weapon IV can support a cautious PvE entry | T2 large weapons are the proper regular-DPS/PvP target | STANDARD support where its SP cost produces a practical benefit |

Battleship III unlocks the hull but leaves one full level of hull bonuses unused. Level IV is therefore the practical target before investing in an expensive regular-use fit. Complete hull IV, tank and fitting support before raising secondary skills to V.

The Dominix and other hulls whose primary weapon is drones are exceptions to the rule that a battleship needs T2 large weapons. On those hulls, the appropriate drone module replaces the turret plan.

- [Amarr](amarr/README-EN.md) — Apocalypse and Abaddon laser branches plus the drone-focused Armageddon.
- [Caldari](caldari/README-EN.md) — Raven missiles, Rokh hybrid turrets and the support-oriented Scorpion.
- [Gallente](gallente/README-EN.md) — Dominix drones plus Megathron and Hyperion hybrid-turret branches.
- [Minmatar](minmatar/README-EN.md) — Maelstrom and Tempest projectile branches plus the missile-focused Typhoon.
