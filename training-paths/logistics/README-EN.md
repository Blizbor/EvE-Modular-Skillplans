# Logistics

[🇵🇱 Polski](README-PL.md) | 🇬🇧 **English**

Each document describes the four available logistics stages directly: T1 Logistics Frigate, T2 Logistics Frigate, T1 Logistics Cruiser and T2 Logistics Cruiser. Fleet composition and doctrines remain outside this project.

- [Amarr](amarr/README-EN.md) — Inquisitor, Deacon, Augoror and Guardian; armor repairs, with capacitor transfer on the cruiser line.
- [Caldari](caldari/README-EN.md) — Bantam, Kirin, Osprey and Basilisk; shield repairs, with capacitor transfer on the cruiser line.
- [Gallente](gallente/README-EN.md) — Navitas, Thalia, Exequror and Oneiros; armor repairs.
- [Minmatar](minmatar/README-EN.md) — Burst, Scalpel, Scythe and Scimitar; shield repairs and mobility.
