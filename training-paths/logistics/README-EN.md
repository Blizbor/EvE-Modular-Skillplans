# Logistics

[🇵🇱 Polski](README-PL.md) | 🇬🇧 **English**

Each document describes the four available logistics stages directly: T1 Logistics Frigate, T2 Logistics Frigate, T1 Logistics Cruiser and T2 Logistics Cruiser. Fleet composition and doctrines remain outside this project.

Logistics is a skill-intensive role. Each racial path therefore provides separate ENTRY, REGULAR and SPECIALIST training strings where the distinction is useful:

- **ENTRY** unlocks the hull or tool and supports first trials;
- **REGULAR** raises the relevant logistics hull skill and tools to IV;
- **SPECIALIST** uses level V where it directly improves repair output, capacitor economics or hull bonuses.

## Why the intermediate stages matter

| Element | ENTRY | REGULAR | SPECIALIST |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 racial hull | level III supports first trials | level IV applies another level of repair or range bonuses | level V only before the relevant T2 hull or for deliberate specialization |
| Logistics Frigates/Cruisers | level III unlocks the T2 hull | level IV is the practical minimum for regular use | level V applies another full level of strong hull bonuses |
| Remote repair | skill III supports entry and small T2 modules | skill IV unlocks medium/large T2 modules and reduces capacitor need by another 5% | skill V reduces capacitor need by a further 5% |
| Repair drones | Repair Drone Operation III | level IV provides 5% more repair amount | level V provides another 5% |
| Capacitor transfer | level III permits initial use | level IV unlocks medium/large T2 transmitters and is the regular cap-chain minimum | level V further reduces capacitor cost |

Logistics does not make a single jump from “not working” to “complete.” BASIC lets the pilot begin learning position and capacitor management, IMPROVED opens regular T2 modules and hull IV, and STANDARD improves performance on every cycle throughout the flight.

Amarr and Caldari cruisers also use capacitor transfer. A low level in that tool affects not just the pilot's own capacitor but the stability of the entire cap chain. Gallente and Minmatar do not receive that module automatically because their primary path does not require a cap chain.

The tool modules are `40` Remote Armor Repair, `41` Remote Shield Boost, `42` Capacitor Transfer and `43` Repair Drones. Copy only the contents of the fenced block below `🅰️Training string:` or `⭐Training string:` and pass it to the plan mixer as its single argument.

- [Amarr](amarr/README-EN.md) — Inquisitor, Deacon, Augoror and Guardian; armor repairs, with capacitor transfer on the cruiser line.
- [Caldari](caldari/README-EN.md) — Bantam, Kirin, Osprey and Basilisk; shield repairs, with capacitor transfer on the cruiser line.
- [Gallente](gallente/README-EN.md) — Navitas, Thalia, Exequror and Oneiros; armor repairs.
- [Minmatar](minmatar/README-EN.md) — Burst, Scalpel, Scythe and Scimitar; shield repairs and mobility.
