## T2 turrets

T2 small, medium and large Energy/Hybrid/Projectile turrets require the corresponding turret skill at V plus the relevant specialization at I. In this library the weapon-family progression is:

- `51/52/53 BASIC` — small T2 weapons,
- `51/52/53 IMPROVED` — medium T2 weapons,
- `51/52/53 STANDARD` — large T2 weapons and the mature specialization level defined by the plan.
