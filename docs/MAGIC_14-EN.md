# EvE Modular Skillplans vs “Magic 14”

[🇵🇱 Polski](MAGIC_14-PL.md) | 🇬🇧 **English**

**Magic 14** is a set of highly universal support skills that improve almost every ship. They are valuable, but by themselves they do not form a complete route to a specific hull, weapon system, tank or role.

EvE Modular Skillplans starts where such a list stops being enough.

- **Magic 14 asks:** “Which skills are useful almost everywhere?”
- **Modular Skillplans asks:** “What exactly do I need for this ship and role — and where is a sensible stopping point?”

## Key differences

1. **A goal instead of a universal character.** A shield/missile pilot is not forced to train armor and lasers.
2. **More than core skills.** Plans include Weapon Upgrades/AWU, Thermodynamics, specific tanks, weapons, rigging, drones, scouting, cynos and hull progression.
3. **Explicit levels.** The ordered levels `BASIC`, `IMPROVED` and `STANDARD` explain not only what to train, but when to stop.
4. **Level V needs a reason.** There is no automatic “train everything to V”.
5. **Training Paths.** Modules can be assembled into routes toward HACs, Battleships, Gila, Logistics, Mining and other concrete goals.
6. **Usable fits come first.** CPU, powergrid, capacitor, T2 requirements and real hull bonuses matter more than the number of completed skills.

Magic 14 remains a good foundation. This project does not try to replace that idea; it expands universal supports into a complete modular pilot-development system.
