# Not everything belongs at level V

[🇵🇱 Polski](NEVER_LEVEL_5-PL.md) | 🇬🇧 **English**

Read this as **“never automatically to V in a corp plan”**, not as a literal ban on training level V.

## Rule

Level V belongs in `STANDARD` when it:

- unlocks a module, hull or further skill promised by the plan,
- is a prerequisite for further progression, or
- has unusually high value for the specific role.

Other V levels are deliberate pilot choices after reaching `STANDARD`.

## Important exceptions

`00 CORE - Engineering, Capacitor & Rigging` deliberately takes these to V in `STANDARD`:

- CPU Management V
- Power Grid Management V
- Capacitor Management V
- Capacitor Systems Operation V

They are fundamental to real fitting and capacitor performance and often separate a sensible fit from expensive modules or poor compromises.

`Drones V` is another normal exception because it provides the fifth drone and unlocks important further progression.

## Common intentional stops at IV

### Fitting and weapons
- Advanced Weapon Upgrades IV — V when a real fit or prerequisite requires it.
- Weapon specializations usually stop at IV; specialization V is not a default target.
- Turret and missile support skills usually stop at IV unless V is required for the promised T2 access.

### Navigation
IV is commonly enough for Navigation, Evasive Maneuvering, Warp Drive Operation, Afterburner, Acceleration Control, High Speed Maneuvering and Fuel Conservation.

Afterburner V **does not worsen average capacitor use**: its shorter cycle is offset by the lower activation cost. IV is simply a reasonable stop when other SP provide more value.

### Targeting and heat
Long Range Targeting, Signature Analysis, Thermodynamics, Nanite Operation and Nanite Interfacing commonly stop at IV. Logistics, EWAR or sniper roles may justify V.

### Armor
Common IV stops include racial Armor Compensation skills, Armor Layering, Armor Rigging, Repair Systems and Resistance Phasing.

`Repair Systems V` increases repair per second by shortening the cycle, but cap per activation does not fall, so capacitor use per second also rises. This is a real trade-off and should be a deliberate choice.

`Hull Upgrades V` is not treated as an unnecessary V because it unlocks important T2 armor modules.

### Shield
Natural stops are often Shield Operation IV, Shield Management IV, Shield Upgrades IV, Tactical Shield Manipulation IV, Shield Compensation IV and Shield Rigging IV. Go higher for a specific module, tank or role.

### Scanning / covert
Astrometrics, the three Astrometric support skills, Cloaking and Covert Ops commonly stop at IV unless a later prerequisite requires V.

### Hull skills
Spaceship Command V or racial hull V are completely appropriate when they unlock the next hull/T2 class or form an important part of a specific training path.

## Why this matters

`STANDARD` should describe a **well-trained pilot**, not a character that blindly maximizes every support skill. After reaching STANDARD, the pilot should understand the role well enough to choose which additional V levels are worth the time.
